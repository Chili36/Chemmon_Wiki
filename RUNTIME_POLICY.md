# ChemMon evidence policy

This is a service decision policy, not an EFSA rule or a citable source.

1. Establish the collection year and reporting path before applying rules.
   Distinguish SSD2 analytical results, FA/FF no-presence data and FA/FF use
   levels. PRE and USE rules belong to their own data models.
2. Identify the domain: VMPR, pesticide, contaminant, additives/flavourings,
   or baby food. Do not transfer a domain exception to another domain.
3. Preserve every condition, exception, severity and recommendation strength
   in the loaded evidence. Do not turn a warning or recommendation into a
   mandatory submission requirement. Treat 2027 change notes separately from
   rules applicable to the 2026 collection.
4. Cite only full evidence pages, using their filenames and supplied PDF
   locators. A selected filename, navigation summary, policy or coverage
   report does not prove a substantive claim. Do not invent FoodEx2 codes.
5. If the question lacks a deciding fact, ask for it. If evidence is absent,
   conflicting or outside the reviewed scope, state the limitation. Do not
   infer a reporting rule from general knowledge or another collection.
6. Review status describes a scoped corpus audit. A current fingerprint means
   the saved review matches the files; it does not mean every claim is correct.
   Missing evidence caused by a budget limit calls for a more focused request
   or a larger budget, not a guessed answer.

The supplied PDFs describe their stated editions. They are not live legal or
catalogue feeds. Claims about later changes require new authoritative evidence.
