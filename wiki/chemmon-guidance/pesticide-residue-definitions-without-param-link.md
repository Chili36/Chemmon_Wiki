## How to use this list

- **Two different situations.** A **confirmed catalogue gap** means the legal residue definition and the reportable PARAM terms do not match, and rd-bridge records the legal source and the related terms. **No confirmed PARAM link** means only that rd-bridge could not confirm a match in PARAM 17.8. This is **not evidence that PARAM has no code**: the cause is often different wording, a broader or narrower term, or a substance outside the pesticide hierarchy.
- **Do not substitute a similar code.** A term that only resembles the definition, such as a broader isomer sum or the individual substance, is not the whole-residue code. Look for the definition in the `ChemMonRep` reporting hierarchy and EFSA's Legal Limits database ([[legal-limits-database]]), and see [[ssd2-analysis-parameter-coding]] for `paramCode` rules. Where no matching code exists, ask EFSA instead of choosing a near match.
- **Coverage.** This page lists 1 confirmed catalogue gap and 141 scopes without a confirmed link, out of 767 residue-definition scopes in rd-bridge snapshot `b4e9020` (residue map of 2026-10-14). Scopes not listed have a confirmed whole-residue PARAM link; see [[pesticide-residue-definitions-by-food]] for definitions that change by food.
- **Latest definitions only.** The snapshot holds the latest EU-published definitions. A definition that applies from a future date, or that applied to older samples, may differ; check the legal source and the sampling date.
- **Authority.** rd-bridge is a derived, non-official bridge. The legislation decides; EFSA validates pesticide `paramCode`s against the Legal Limits database.

## Confirmed catalogue gaps

### Benthiavalicarb-isopropyl (Default)

- **Legal residue definition:** Benthiavalicarb-isopropyl (sum of benthiavalicarb-isopropyl (KIF 230 R-L) and its RS enantiomer (KIF 230-S-D))
- **Legal source:** https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/147/oj/eng, applies from 2026-08-12
- **Gap in PARAM 17.8:** EU Regulation 2026/147 defines the enforcement residue as KIF-230 R-L plus KIF-230 S-D. The available reportable PARAM term also includes KIF-230 S-L and KIF-230 R-D.
- **Related PARAM terms:**
  - `RF-00004656-PAR` Benthiavalicarb (Benthiavalicarb-isopropyl(KIF-230 R-L) and its enantiomer (KIF-230 S-D) and its diastereomers(KIF-230 S-L and KIF-230 R-D), expressed as benthiavalicarb-isopropyl): broader four isomer residue, **not an exact match**.
  - `RF-0043-003-PPP` Benthiavalicarb isopropyl: nonreportable individual substance, **not an exact match**.
- **What to do:** Use an updated EFSA code for this legal residue definition when one exists, or explicit EFSA reporting guidance confirming a code. Do not treat either related code as an exact whole-definition match.
