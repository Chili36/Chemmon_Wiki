# ChemMon Change Management

<!-- Source: EFSA Supporting Publications - 2026 -  - Chemical monitoring reporting guidance  2026 data collection.pdf pp. 10-16 -->

## Rule Summary

- Ordinary annual changes proposed in October-November of year X usually apply to results reported in year X+2.
- The X+2 delay exists because sampling officers need requirements before samples are collected.
- The X+2 timing does not apply to changes implementing new or amended legal requirements that EFSA cannot anticipate.
- The 2026 change table classifies changes by legal requirement, quality requirement, or sampling requirement.

## Revision Cadence

For national annual monitoring, changes proposed to elements, catalogues, or business rules in October-November of year X can generally only be applied to results reported in year X+2. (ChemMon 2026 p10)

This delay exists because changes need to be known before sampling officers collect samples in year X+1. A new facet or element requirement cannot fairly be applied to samples that were already taken before the rule existed. (ChemMon 2026 p10)

The exception is a change required by new or amended legislation that could not be anticipated by EFSA. The guidance says only that the X+2 timing "would not apply" to them. (ChemMon 2026 p10)

Network members must contribute to and participate in the revision process on an ongoing basis, with emphasis on suggestions submitted during the last quarter of each year, (September-November). (ChemMon 2026 p10)

## Main Change Categories for 2026

The 2026 guidance lists major changes in Table 1. They fall into three broad categories:

- **Legal requirement**: new or amended regulations, such as new `progLegalRef` N422A for Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1873 on intensified official controls at border control posts on products of animal origin, germinal products, animal by-products and composite products, added to Table 2. (ChemMon 2026 p11)
- **Quality requirement**: tightened or clarified business rules to improve data quality.
- **Sampling requirement**: addition of sampling strategy ST20A in combination with K009A and K018A for pesticides (`progLegalRef` N027A). (ChemMon 2026 p11)

(ChemMon 2026 pp. 11-16)

**Source discrepancy:** Table 1 says N422A "has been flagged for contaminants, pesticides, VMPR, food additives and food flavourings" and names Regulation (EU) 2019/1873 (ChemMon 2026 p11). The legal-reference list in the annex prints N422A as "Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/1873" with the flags "OCC, PEST, VMPR" only (ChemMon 2026 p146). The rules CHEMON101 and CHEMON104 use "(EU) No 2019/1873" (ChemMon 2026 pp. 138-139).
