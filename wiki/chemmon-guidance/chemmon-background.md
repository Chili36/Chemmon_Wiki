## Overview

This page is the background hub for ChemMon. The source background section explains why EFSA consolidated chemical monitoring into one SSD2-based collection, how overlapping domains are handled, why free text is reduced, and how annual change management works.

Use [[chemmon-overview]] for the operational overview of ChemMon. Use the narrower background pages below when a question is about rationale, legal basis, data governance, or change cadence.
